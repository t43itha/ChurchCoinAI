# Categorisation model screen — 13 August 2026

## Decision

- **Safest near-term upgrade:** Gemini 2.5 Flash for ambiguous AI fallbacks.
- **Best measured value, pending a repeatability/availability gate:** GPT-5.6 Luna.
- **Keep Flash-Lite for:** cheap first-pass classification where memory and rules have
  already removed difficult cases.
- **Do not switch to DeepSeek V4 Flash based on this screen:** it was less accurate
  and materially slower than the current baseline for this workload.

## Method

- 50 synthetic UK church transactions; no production, donor, church, or bank data.
- Five batches of 10 transactions.
- Exact ChurchCoin income categories, expenditure categories, and fund names.
- Strict structured output, provider fallback disabled, and OpenRouter routing set
  to `data_collection: deny`.
- Scores below are a screening result, not a production acceptance test. The first
  matrix is one complete pass. A later two-pass confirmation exceeded the local
  shell limit before it could write a complete report and is excluded.

## Complete results

| Model | Category accuracy | All fields correct | Median latency / 10 | P95 latency / 10 | Observed cost / 1,000* |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| GPT-5.6 Luna | 92% | 90% | 363 ms | 387 ms | $0.039 |
| Gemini 2.5 Flash | 92% | 90% | 587 ms | 698 ms | $0.204 |
| Gemini 2.5 Flash-Lite | 82% | 78% | 455 ms | 506 ms | $0.034 |
| Ministral 14B | 84% | 80% | 289 ms | 959 ms | $0.030 |
| DeepSeek V4 Flash | 76% | 76% | 1,099 ms | 3,803 ms | $0.022 |
| GPT-4o mini | 62% | 58% | 429 ms | 716 ms | $0.043 |
| GPT-OSS 20B | 74% | 72% | 222 ms | 225 ms | $0.014 |

\* Linear projection from five 10-transaction requests, including repeated prompt
tokens. Larger production batches may cost less per transaction.

Flash-Lite also had a separate smoke pass at 86% category / 84% all-fields accuracy,
503 ms median latency, and $0.038 per 1,000 projected. That variation is why a
larger labelled production set is required before changing the live model.

GPT-5 Nano is omitted from the quality table because OpenRouter had no endpoint
that satisfied the strict structured-output and no-data-collection request together.

## Error pattern

The largest shared weakness was restricted-purpose income: Charity Fund was often
confused with Offerings, Mission Support, or Uncategorised. Models also confused
Premises - Manse with Utilities or worship-premises rent. These distinctions need
explicit examples and deterministic rules; a model upgrade alone is insufficient.

## Recommended production experiment

1. Keep exact correction memory and safe deterministic rules first.
2. Use Flash-Lite for ordinary unresolved rows.
3. Escalate low-confidence or restricted-purpose rows to Gemini 2.5 Flash.
4. Shadow-test GPT-5.6 Luna on anonymised production examples before considering it
   as the primary model.
5. Require at least 200 manually labelled, representative transactions, zero schema
   failures, and a pre-agreed accuracy threshold before rollout.
