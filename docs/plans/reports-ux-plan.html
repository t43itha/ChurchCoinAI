Monthly & annual reports: review and redesign plan

The reports compute the right totals but present them as a stack of equal-weight accordions. A treasurer can answer “how much came in?” but not “is that good?”, “what do we owe?” or “how much do we have?” without leaving the page. This plan reorders the page around those questions and fills the gaps with data we mostly already compute.

Verdict: the data is mostly there, the hierarchy is not

Of the ten questions a treasurer, pastor or trustee brings to these reports, the monthly report answers 3 at a glance and the annual 4. The biggest gaps are context (no comparisons on the monthly, a misleading one on the annual), position (fund balances absent from monthly, collapsed on annual) and obligations (loans are computed by the query but never shown on screen or in the PDF).

Findings

Ordered by how much they hurt comprehension. References are to components/Reports.tsx and convex/queries/reports.ts.

HighAnnual YoY compares a part-year to a full year

Viewing 2026 in October compares Jan–Oct 2026 with all of 2025, so income always looks down ~17%. reports.ts:871 has no like-for-like window. This is the one number on the page that is actively wrong to read.

HighMonthly has no context

Four bare totals with no previous month, no same-month-last-year, no trend. “£14,820 income” means nothing without a reference point.

HighLoans are computed but never displayed

Both queries return loans (reports.ts:697, 917) and Excel exports them, but neither the screen nor the PDF renders them. Trustees can't see outstanding debt.

HighFund position is missing or hidden

Monthly shows no balances. Annual's Fund Balances starts collapsed (Reports.tsx:751) and shows only closing balance: no opening, income, spending or transfers per fund, which is what a SORP statement of funds needs.

MediumAnnual ignores the reporting period setting

Organisations default to tax_year (Onboarding.tsx:119) and the AI tab respects it, but the annual report is hard-coded to 1 Jan–31 Dec (reports.ts:711).

MediumEverything has the same visual weight

Ten accordion cards in a row, each with the same header style. The Tithes list (one row per gift, not per donor) is often the longest block on the page and sits above Gift Aid and Programmes.

MediumCategory tables are unsorted and proportion-blind

Groups follow Map insertion order, so the largest category can appear last. No share-of-total, no bar, no change, and the header repeats the KPI total.

MediumSpending is painted red

Expenditure totals, bars and weekly figures use text-error. Paying the rent isn't an error; red should be reserved for deficits and overdrawn funds so it keeps its meaning.

MediumDuplicated and boilerplate blocks

Gift Aid claimable appears as a KPI and again in its own card with static explainer copy. The footer card repeats the church name and period already in the header; it only matters in print.

MediumReports and Dashboard disagree on scope

The Dashboard already computes operating position, giving trend, general fund cover and month-end readiness (lib/dashboardKpis.ts, lib/dashboardChecks.ts), but Reports re-derives totals separately and shows none of these. Mission tithe is computed in both places.

LowSmall correctness and polish issues

“N contributors” counts gifts, not people. Monthly “next” allows future months; annual blocks them. Tab class typo py-3-mb-px (Reports.tsx:1741) drops both padding and the underline offset. Fund and tithe totals use reduce rather than sumMoney.

LowHeader controls don't adapt

Period stepper and two export buttons sit in one non-wrapping row; on narrow screens they overflow. The page title reads as grey caption text while the generic “Reports” banner takes the hero slot.

What changes

Order follows the questions. Verdict → numbers with context → what to do → where money went → where it sits → detail on demand.

Every number gets a reference point. Previous month and same month last year, plus a 12-month sparkline.

Obligations get their own panel. Mission tithe, Gift Aid, loans and data-quality checks become one actionable list instead of four scattered cards.

Detail moves down and closes. Weekly rhythm, giving by donor, programmes and transfers live under “More detail”, closed by default but always in the PDF.

Colour means something. Income and spending use two neutral series colours; red is kept for deficits and overdrawn funds.

Phased plan

Each phase ships on its own and leaves the page better than it found it. Phase 1 needs no backend change.

Phase 1 · UI only

Hierarchy and honesty

~2–3 daysReports.tsx split
  1. Split Reports.tsx (1,778 lines) into components/reports/: shared ReportHeader, KpiCard, RankedBreakdown, ReportSection.
  2. New order; detail sections closed by default.
  3. Ranked category bars with share; subcategories expand in place.
  4. Render loans (screen + PDF).
  5. Fund balances open by default on annual.
  6. Hide the YoY % when the year is incomplete, until phase 2 fixes it.
  7. Neutral series colours; red only for deficits/overdrawn.
  8. Single Export menu; wrapping header; block future months; fix tab typo; sumMoney for totals.
Phase 2 · Queries

Context and position

~3–4 daysconvex/queries/reports.ts
  1. summarisePeriod helper shared by monthly and annual.
  2. Comparisons: previous month, same month last year, like-for-like YoY.
  3. Statement of funds for both reports.
  4. Annual follows reportingPeriod; in-progress label.
  5. Giving grouped by donor id; giver counts.
  6. Tests: like-for-like windows, fund statement balances tie to totals, tax-year boundaries (5/6 April).
Phase 3 · Polish

Summary, actions, outputs

~2–3 daysdashboard reuse
  1. Deterministic headline sentence (pure function in lib/, unit tested).
  2. “To action” panel and readiness chip via a period-aware executiveSummary.
  3. Reserve cover KPI.
  4. Annual trend: prior-year reference line, cumulative and table views.
  5. PDF and Excel follow the new structure (PDF is what trustees actually read).
  6. Mobile pass on a real phone.

Decisions needed

Should the annual report follow the organisation's reporting period?

Today it's calendar-only while the org default is tax year. Many charities have a financial year that is neither (e.g. 1 Jan or 1 Sep).

Recommend: yes, and add a “financial year starts” month setting rather than a fixed tax/calendar switch.
Is Mission Tithe universal or one church's rule?

It's hard-coded (10% of three categories in unrestricted funds) for every tenant.

Recommend: make it an org setting (on/off, rate, categories); hide the row when off.
Do you want budgets?

Budget vs actual is the most common monthly question for trustees, and there is no budget table today. It would slot into the ranked bars as a target marker.

Recommend: separate project after phase 2; the layout leaves room for it.
Reserves target

Reserve cover is only useful against a policy. Most small churches aim for 3–6 months of unrestricted spending.

Recommend: default 3 months, editable in Settings.
